Case 6 — Max Yaw Rate

case6_max_yaw · generated 2026-08-05 19:09

Units and sign conventions read before interpreting any number here
  • Yaw rate positive = rotating LEFT (counter-clockwise seen from above), matching case1's lateral-G convention. Established by cross-reference against lateral G (r = +0.96 to +0.99), not assumed.
  • Corner radius is R = v/ω — the radius of the line the car is actually following, which is not the painted radius of the corner. The difference is the driver's line.
  • This channel is validated every run, not trusted from a note. The identity a_lat = v·ω shares no sensor with the gyro, so the per-file r and slope in the validation section say whether the yaw channel is still sound. A slope above 1.0 is expected — the identity assumes zero sideslip and wheel speed under-reads in a corner.
  • Nothing here is integrated. Yaw rate has a 7–18% scale error that is harmless instantaneously and fatal once accumulated — it is why dead-reckoning a track map fails. Every number on this page is an instantaneous value or a ratio of two, never an integral.
  • The roll and pitch channels of this same sensor are NOT usable: chassis roll is 0.82 °/s RMS against a 0.45 °/s noise floor, while yaw is 49.6 against 0.36. Use the shock pots for roll and pitch.
