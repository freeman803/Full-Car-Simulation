Units and sign conventions read before interpreting any number here
  • Per-corner wheel travel, relative to that corner's own stopped-car baseline. negative = COMPRESSION (bump), positive = EXTENSION (droop). Both have a mechanical limit, so both extremes are reported.
  • These are wheel millimetres, not shock-pot millimetres — the motion ratio (1.188 front, 1.038 rear) is applied per corner before any roll/pitch/modal arithmetic.
  • heave = all four corners moving together (no attitude change). roll = left pair vs right pair. pitch = front pair vs rear pair. warp = the diagonals opposing each other — the chassis being twisted. Each mode's mm is its contribution to one corner, and the four sum exactly to that corner's travel.
  • Longitudinal G positive = slowing down (verified empirically against vehicle speed, not assumed). Lateral G positive = cornering left.
  • A peak flagged NOT A DRIVING EVENT happened below 3 m/s. The motion is real, but it is not a load the car saw on track.
