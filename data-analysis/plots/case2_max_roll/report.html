Case 2 — Max Roll

case2_max_roll · generated 2026-08-05 19:07

Units and sign conventions read before interpreting any number here
  • Roll is right-side travel minus left-side travel, and a HIGHER mm reading is more EXTENSION on this car. So roll > 0 = right side extended, LEFT side compressed — the opposite of what the formula suggests at a glance. Verified on skidpad: correlation with lateral G is −0.998.
  • These are wheel millimetres — the motion ratio (1.188 front, 1.038 rear) is applied per corner before the difference is taken, because the two ratios differ.
  • The whole-car avg angle converts mean-mm over mean-track rather than averaging two separately-converted angles. The error is 0.17–0.19% and is documented rather than fixed, so published numbers stay comparable.
  • Front and rear roll disagree by 7–27% on this car for reasons not yet explained — see the README's known data problems. Two autocross runs additionally have unusable front pots and are flagged in place below.
  • A peak is one instantaneous sample of the filtered trace. The bracketed 0.2s mean next to it says whether that instant was a sustained plateau or an isolated spike.
