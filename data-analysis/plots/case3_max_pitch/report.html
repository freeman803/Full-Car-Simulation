Units and sign conventions read before interpreting any number here
  • Pitch is front-axle average minus rear-axle average. A HIGHER mm reading is more EXTENSION on this car, so pitch > 0 = the orientation seen under ACCELERATION (squat) and pitch < 0 = BRAKING (dive). This is the opposite of a 'positive = nose-down' reading — verified empirically against vehicle speed, not assumed.
  • These are wheel millimetres. The motion ratio matters more here than anywhere else: pitch IS the front-minus-rear difference, so the per-corner ratios (1.188 front, 1.038 rear) must be applied before the subtraction, not after.
  • Braking windows come from front brake pressure (>100 psi) AND speed (>3 m/s), not from thresholding longitudinal G. Pressure is the driver's input; lon G is only the result, and also responds to drivetrain drag and gradient. Green shading on the brake plots marks the detected windows.
  • VCFRONT_brakePressure is sampled at 10 Hz, so its Nyquist is 5 Hz — it is filtered at 3 Hz for that reason.
  • A peak is one instantaneous sample of the filtered trace. The bracketed 0.2s mean next to it says whether that instant was a sustained plateau or an isolated spike.
