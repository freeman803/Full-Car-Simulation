Units and sign conventions read before interpreting any number here
  • Lateral G positive = cornering left. Longitudinal G positive = slowing down — verified empirically against vehicle speed in case3, not assumed from the signal name.
  • G's come from VCPDU_lat / VCPDU_lon, which are in m/s² in the raw data and divided by 9.80665 here. Any number on this page is already in g.
  • Skidpad is a sustained measurement — a median over the steady circling segments — so it has no separate 'peak' by design. The other events report peaks.
  • A peak is one instantaneous sample of the filtered trace. The bracketed 0.2s mean next to it says whether that instant was a sustained plateau or an isolated spike.
  • alpha is the direction of the g-g vector at that instant — atan2(|lat|, |lon|) in degrees, i.e. where the point sits on the friction circle. 90° = pure cornering (no braking or acceleration), 0° = pure straight-line braking or acceleration, 45° = equal parts of both, the combined-load corner of the circle. So lat −1.60 g, lon +0.14 g, alpha 85° was essentially pure lateral grip — cornering, not trail-braking. It tells you at a glance whether a peak is a single-axis event or a genuine combined-load one.
